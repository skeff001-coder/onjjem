### How the Contrado → Shopify flow works
1. **Design each product in Contrado** (upload artwork / set it up on the Contrado product you want).
2. In Shopify, install the **"Contrado: Print on Demand"** app (Shopify App Store) and connect it to your Contrado account — you only sync once.
3. Turn on **Auto-Publish** in the Contrado app (My Stores tab) so every new Contrado product appears in Shopify automatically (otherwise they land in Shopify as **Drafts** and you publish them manually).
4. Set your **retail price** = your website price (you can let Contrado manage pricing, or set it in Shopify per Part 1).
5. **Tell me the new Shopify product names/handles** and I will wire up their "Buy Now" buttons on the website. Until then they stay as "Email Us to Order".

> Important: Contrado only auto-fulfils through **Shopify**. So every product you want to sell hands-free must end up in your Shopify store.
