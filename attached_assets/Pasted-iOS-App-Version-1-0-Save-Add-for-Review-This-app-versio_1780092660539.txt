Business model clarification (Guideline 2.1b):

1. Who are the users that will use the paid subscriptions?
Users who upload family photographs to receive AI-powered restoration — sharpening blurry or low-resolution photos, and adding colour to black-and-white images.
This app does NOT use any third-party AI service. All photo enhancements (sharpening, colour restoration, denoising) are performed using Sharp, an open-source library running entirely on ONJJEM's own server at onjjem.com. No photo or user data is ever sent to OpenAI, Replicate, Google, or any external AI provider. Full details are in our privacy policy at https://onjjem.com/privacy.
Two auto-renewable subscriptions (ONJJEM Pro Monthly £11.99/mo and ONJJEM Pro Annual £24.99/yr) are configured in the ONJJEM PRO subscription group and are Ready to Submit. The In-App Purchase Key has been configured in RevenueCat. To reach the paywall: tap "Enhance Photo", select any photo from your library, then tap the gold "Get Pro Access" button when the result appears.


2. Where can users purchase the subscriptions?
Exclusively through Apple In-App Purchase. Product IDs: com.onjjem.photorestoration.monthly and com.onjjem.photorestoration.annual. No purchase can be made outside the app.

3. What specific types of previously purchased subscriptions can a user access?
An active subscription unlocks unlimited AI photo restorations (Sharpen and Colourisation). Without a subscription, users may process one photo free to evaluate quality before purchasing.

4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app that do not use In-App Purchase?
None. All digital features requiring a subscription use Apple In-App Purchase exclusively.

The app also displays a catalogue of physical goods (canvas prints, cushions, candles). The in-app screen is an order request only — no payment is collected in-app and no card details are entered. Our team contacts the user to arrange payment and delivery offline. The screen now reads "No Payment Taken Here".

5. Can users purchase physical goods together with digital content in your app?
No. The digital AI restoration subscription (via Apple IAP) and the physical goods catalogue are entirely separate. Physical goods are handled as an offline enquiry only. There is no bundled purchase of physical and digital content.
