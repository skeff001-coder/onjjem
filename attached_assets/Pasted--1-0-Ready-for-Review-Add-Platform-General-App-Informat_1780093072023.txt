Business model clarification (Guideline 2.1b):

This app does NOT use any third-party AI service. All photo enhancements (sharpening, colour restoration, denoising) are performed with Sharp, an open-source image library running entirely on ONJJEM's own server. No photo or user data is ever sent to OpenAI, Replicate, Google, or any external provider. Privacy policy: https://onjjem.com/privacy

1. Who uses the paid purchases?
People restoring family photographs — sharpening blurry/low-resolution photos and adding colour to black-and-white images. The app gives one free photo to evaluate quality. After that there are two ways to pay, both via Apple In-App Purchase:
• One Photo Enhancement — £1.49 one-time (Consumable). Product ID: com.onjjem.photorestoration.onephoto
• Monthly Unlimited — £12.99/month auto-renewable subscription (group "ONJJEM Pro Access"). Product ID: com.onjjem.photorestoration.pro.monthly2

To reach the paywall: tap "Enhance Photo" → pick a photo → run an enhancement. Once the free photo is used, the paywall appears (gold "Get Pro Access" button).

2. Where can users purchase?
Exclusively through Apple In-App Purchase, inside the app. No purchases are made outside the app.

3. What does a purchase unlock?
The monthly subscription unlocks unlimited restorations (Sharpen and Colourise). The £1.49 one-time purchase unlocks a single restoration. The first photo is free.

4. Any paid digital features NOT using In-App Purchase?
None. All digital features requiring payment use Apple In-App Purchase exclusively.
The app also shows a catalogue of physical goods (canvas prints, cushions, candles). That screen is an order ENQUIRY only — no payment is taken in-app and no card details are entered ("No Payment Taken Here"). Our team contacts the customer to arrange payment and delivery offline.

5. Can users buy physical goods together with digital content?
No. The digital purchases (Apple IAP) and the physical-goods catalogue are completely separate. There is no bundled purchase.
