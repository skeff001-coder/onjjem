Subject Strategic Fulfilment Partnership Proposal: ONJJEM × Bags of Love
Dear Wholesale Partnerships Team,

I am writing to propose a formal primary fulfilment partnership between Bags of Love (Contrado Imaging Ltd) and ONJJEM — a high-growth, AI-powered photo restoration platform built for the luxury consumer market, launching in the United Kingdom this quarter.

ONJJEM enables everyday consumers to breathe life back into their most precious photographic memories — faded family portraits, wartime photographs, cracked and discoloured slides — transforming them into museum-quality, print-ready restorations using proprietary Cinema-Grade AI. After extensively vetting print facilities across the UK, we have selected Bags of Love as our preferred fulfilment house, on the basis of your superior craftsmanship, colour consistency, and London-based logistics infrastructure.

What ONJJEM Does

Our platform operates at the intersection of artificial intelligence and emotional heritage. Users upload a photograph directly from their iPhone — whether a blurred, faded, or black-and-white image — and within moments receive a cinema-grade AI restoration: sharpened, denoised, colourised and dramatically enhanced. They may then order a range of luxury physical products — from archival fine art prints and premium canvas mounts to personalised wallpaper murals — delivered to their door.

Every restoration order placed through our platform translates directly into a print fulfilment order at your facility. Bags of Love would serve as the exclusive physical production partner for all UK orders.

Scale & Market Positioning

ONJJEM is designed for rapid and sustained scaling. Our go-to-market strategy combines a high-frequency social media campaign targeting emotionally resonant audiences (family nostalgia, genealogy communities, wartime heritage groups) with a performance marketing funnel expected to drive significant order volume from launch week. We are seeking a production partner with the capacity and quality standards to grow alongside us.

The market for personalised photo gifts in the UK is valued at over £1.3 billion annually. ONJJEM occupies a premium, differentiated position within that market: our customers are not purchasing off-the-shelf photo books — they are commissioning restorations of irreplaceable family heirlooms. Average order values reflect this premium intent.

Trade Account & Commercial Terms

To facilitate our projected order volume and maintain alignment with our billing cycles, we respectfully request the establishment of a 30-day Trade Credit Account (Net 30 terms). This structure will ensure a seamless, automated supply chain as we scale, and allow our fulfilment pipeline to operate without friction during high-volume periods.

We are open to discussing minimum monthly volume commitments and would welcome the opportunity to arrange a formal call with your accounts team to agree terms.
